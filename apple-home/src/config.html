    Enable Apple Home Control in Index actions after setup. Device and scene names come from Apple Home. Unlocking a door requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your iPhone passcode while Pebble is open. Scenes that control locks, garage doors, or security systems cannot run here.

    Apple Home access is managed in iOS Settings. Disconnecting here stops this plugin’s access; it does not change your iOS permission or remove any accessories.
