    Codex on your computers (optional)

    Your computer needs Codex remote control on: in the Codex app, or with codex remote-control start.

    1. Copy this link and open it in your phone’s browser. Sign in when asked (OpenAI asks again even if you are signed in).

    2. The browser ends on a page that won’t load, at an address starting with http://localhost:1455. Copy that whole address and paste it here.

      Pair each computer with this phone once: in the Codex app open remote control (Settings → Connections), or run codex remote-control pair, then paste the code it shows (or the QR code’s text).
