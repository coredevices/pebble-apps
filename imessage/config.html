iMessage

This first prototype sends plain-text iMessages to one person. It does not receive messages, sync conversations, send attachments, or support groups.

Use a token generated locally from your own Mac. The hardware-only ExtractKey schema is enough for this build to create its APNs identity and generate fresh Apple validation data immediately before the IDS delegate and registration requests. Legacy validationData is ignored; a compatible pushBootstrap may be used once during import.

Your password is used only for this sign-in attempt and is not saved.
